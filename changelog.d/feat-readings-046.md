### Added

- Each topic's daily reading is now written by its persona to a word target that grows with the
  day's new cards, and it is stored only after every gate has passed: complete, roster, anchors,
  length band, no list markers and the persona's contract. A reading that fails a gate is written
  once more with the gate named, and a second failure stores nothing. With no AI route configured,
  every topic ends as `ai_route_absent` and nothing is sent anywhere.
- Every attempt is recorded, with its turns, tokens and outcome, and kept for ninety days.
