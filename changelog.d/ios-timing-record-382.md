### Added
- Every Apple CI run records its own timing and uploads it whatever the outcome: the planted card suite is timed around its unchanged command, every other `xcodebuild` build, test and archive prints its build timing summary, each static-library target writes its own seconds beside the total with cargo's timings report, the first iPhone simulator boots in a timed step of its own, the framework job pins the Xcode the other jobs pin, and the framework and harness jobs each upload a `timing.json` record (`ci-timing/1`) as `timing-xcframework` and `timing-harness` (SPEC-382).
