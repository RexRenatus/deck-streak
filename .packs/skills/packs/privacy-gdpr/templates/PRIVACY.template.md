# DeckStreak privacy policy

DeckStreak keeps only what it needs to run your study. This table is the whole of it: one row per
category in privacy.json, each naming its lawful basis and how long it is kept.

| category | what | purpose | lawful basis | retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `account` | your Telegram user id, first name and language | signing you in | contract | until account deletion |
| `study-history` | your review sessions | scheduling your reviews | contract | 2 years |
| `preferences` | the settings you chose | remembering your settings | consent | until consent withdrawal |

Backups keep erased data for at most 2 days before they age out.
Service logs are kept for 14 days and never record your name or username.

Send /privacy to the bot to read this policy, /export for a JSON copy of your data, and /delete to
erase it. DeckStreak never uses your data to train a model.
