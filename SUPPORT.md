# Support

## Where to ask

- Questions and ideas: [Discussions](https://github.com/RexRenatus/deck-streak/discussions).
- Bugs and feature requests: the issue forms, under **Issues**, then **New issue**.
- Security problems: privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes. Never in a public issue.

## What to include

The version or commit you ran, what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
